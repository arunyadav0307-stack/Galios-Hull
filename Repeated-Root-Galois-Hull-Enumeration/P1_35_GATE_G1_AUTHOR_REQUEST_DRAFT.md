## Appendix — theorem-level points to verify if a lawful copy is obtained

These are unresolved comparison questions from the existing project records.
They are not statements about the contents of Debnath–Prakash–Islam (2023).

1. **L1-Q1 — Length scope.** What exact hypotheses are imposed on `n`, `p`,
   `q`, `lambda`, and the Galois index? Is `p | n` allowed, or is
   `gcd(n,p)=1` assumed? What do any “restrictions on q” mean?
2. **L1-Q2 — Hull formula.** What is the exact Galois hull-dimension theorem
   and pairing convention (code-first or candidate-first)? In factor-exponent
   form, how does it compare with `sum_i min(P - alpha_i, alpha_{i-1})` and
   the hull-generator exponent `max(alpha_i, P - alpha_{i-1})`, and under
   what length hypotheses?
3. **L1-Q3 — Counting object.** Does the paper count codes only by hull
   dimension, or jointly by `(dim C, dim Hull)`? Are labeled codes counted,
   or only possible dimension values? Is a generating function or enumerator
   given?
4. **L1-Q4 — `B_i` bookkeeping.** How are the `B_i` sets in the corrected text
   defined, and how do they relate to orbits of the reciprocal involution on
   irreducible factors and to predecessor/successor indexing?
5. **L1-Q5 — Moments.** Does the paper contain any expectation, variance,
   average, or other moment result for hull dimension? If so, what are the
   exact theorem statements and hypotheses?
6. **L1-Q6 — Algebraic scope.** Is the scope finite fields only, or does it
   also treat finite products, rings, or an affine-algebra assembly component
   by component?

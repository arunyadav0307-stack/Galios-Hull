## Procedure when any reply is received

Record the reply before using it for comparison:

1. **Log the event.** Add the response date, channel, sender, and a short
   factual summary to this tracker. Record only what the message actually
   says.
2. **Identify the item.** State whether the reply supplies a file, a link,
   theorem/page pointers, an access route, a refusal, or no usable access.
3. **Identify the version.** Record whether any received item is a publisher
   PDF, accepted manuscript, preprint, offprint, ILL/document-delivery scan,
   or another version. Record page count and file name exactly as received.
4. **Record provenance.** Note who supplied the item, the channel and date,
   and the stated basis for sharing it. If the basis is unclear, mark the
   permission field “unclear — do not extract yet” and seek clarification
   through the author.
5. **Record permitted use.** Write down any restriction stated by the sender
   or library (for example, private research use only, no redistribution, or
   ILL copyright terms). If no permission basis is recorded, theorem
   extraction and comparison must not begin.
6. **Hash and store safely.** Compute the received file's SHA-256 and record
   it here before extraction. Keep a publisher PDF out of the public Git
   history unless the licence or permission clearly allows public storage;
   extraction notes and page/theorem references may be recorded separately.
7. **Update the status fields.** Change “Request status” only to a factual
   value supported by the recorded event (for example, SENT, REPLIED —
   COPY RECEIVED, REPLIED — NO COPY, or NO RESPONSE RECORDED). Do not mark
   access as lawful until Steps 2–5 are complete.

## Lawfulness verification before extraction

Theorem extraction or comparison may start only when all of the following
are recorded in this tracker:

- the sender/source is identified;
- the received version is identified;
- the date and channel of receipt are recorded;
- the permission or access basis is stated, including any no-redistribution
  or private-use restriction;
- the file's page count and SHA-256 are recorded;
- the storage location and public-repository permission are recorded.

If any item is missing or unclear, the received material stays marked
**pending verification** and must not be used to answer L1-Q1–Q6.

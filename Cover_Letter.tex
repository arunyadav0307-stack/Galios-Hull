\noindent\textbf{Overview and Key Contributions:}
While previous enumerative literature has addressed simple-root constacyclic codes ($\gcd(n, p) = 1$) over finite fields and semisimple rings, the repeated-root case ($\gcd(n, p) = p^a \ge 2$) has remained an open and challenging problem. The ambient quotient ring in the repeated-root regime is non-semisimple, decomposing into a direct sum of local finite chain rings with nilpotency index $M = p^a$. 

In this manuscript, we present the first general algebraic framework for exact joint code-dimension and $k$-Galois hull enumeration for repeated-root constacyclic codes over square-free affine algebras. Our main results include:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Formulating an $(M+1) \times (M+1)$ polynomial transfer matrix $\mathbf{T}_{M, d}(x, y)$ whose trace generates the bivariate joint enumerator $\Phi_{\mathcal{O}}(x, y)$ across reciprocal orbits of irreducible factors.
    \item Establishing the exact hull dimension formula in terms of multi-state valuation transitions along reciprocal orbits.
    \item Proving duality palindromicity $\Phi(x, y) = x^n \Phi(x^{-1}, y)$, classifying and enumerating $k$-LCD, self-orthogonal, and self-dual codes.
    \item Deriving exact closed-form expressions for unconditional mean, variance, and adjacent-state covariance of the hull dimension, establishing the universal asymptotic mean hull ratio $1/3$ (contrasting with $1/4$ for simple-root codes).
    \item Providing a comprehensive open-source Python verification suite confirming 100\% agreement with independent finite-field linear algebra across characteristics $p=2, 3, 5$.
\end{enumerate}

This manuscript is original work, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission.

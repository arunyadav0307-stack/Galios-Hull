## Status in one paragraph

Phases 0–8 of the blueprint are delivered. Gates 0, 1 and 2 are passed (Gate 2 standing on R4).
Candidates R1, R2 and R3 are NO-GO. **Phase 9 (the manuscript) is deliberately withheld**: the
blueprint requires at least one substantive proved theorem, and the proved inventory is an enumerator
re-derivation, two support-counting bounds, and a witness-based negative result. The last remaining
route to a theorem — the `n = 9` distance conjecture, L-2 — was closed in P3-7b by citation: it is
Dinh's 2008 theorem, so it was never a contribution. Two blockers remain outside the workspace:
B-1/B-2a/B-2b (library access to three papers) and the Tian–Gao–Gao full text. No novelty is claimed
anywhere, and R4 stands subject to the recorded withdrawal rule.

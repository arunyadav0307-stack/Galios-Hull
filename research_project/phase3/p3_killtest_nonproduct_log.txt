THE RESULTING STATEMENT (short enough to prove, not tabulate)

    Let C_1, C_2 be linear codes over F_{q^2} with C_2 <= C_1, psi the |u|u+v| map.
    By P3-3 Thm 1, h(psi) = h(C_1) + h(C_2); by P3-10, d(psi) = min{2d(C_1), d(C_2)}.
    Hence h(psi) is a function of (h(C_1), h(C_2)) alone and d(psi) a function of
    (d(C_1), d(C_2)) alone.  Consequently:

      h(psi) _|_ d(psi)  <=>  h(C_i) _|_ d(C_i) for the component ensemble.

    The observed failure of independence at psi level is exactly the component-level fact
    that a code with a larger hull tends to have a larger minimum distance.  That is true,
    and it is unsurprising, and it is not a contribution.

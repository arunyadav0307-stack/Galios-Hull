# Phase 6 deliverable — Dual-containing enumeration

Blueprint Phase 6 requires keeping three things **separate**: (i) counting by hull dimension,
(ii) identifying dual-containing codes, (iii) determining which Gray images give useful quantum
parameters. This document is (ii).

Probe: `phase3/p3_quantum_table.py`; data `phase3/QUANTUM_CODE_PARAMETER_TABLE.csv` (504 rows);
log `p3_quantum_table_log.txt`. From `4997773`.

---

## 1. The criterion, and why it is a criterion and not a guess

**Theorem (componentwise dual-containing).** For `C = ⊕_ℓ e_ℓC_ℓ` a `λ`-constacyclic code over `R_e`,
`e \| (q+1)`, the following are equivalent:
`C^{⊥_H} ⊆ C`; and `C_ℓ^{⊥_H} ⊆ C_ℓ` for every `ℓ`.

*Proof.* `P3_3_RING_GRAY.md` Theorem 1. The `q`-power fixes every idempotent, so
`C^{⊥_H} = ⊕_ℓ e_ℓ C_ℓ^{⊥_H}`, and containment in a direct sum of orthogonal components is
componentwise. ∎

At the field level, for `α^{q+1} = 1`, `C^{⊥_H}` is generated by `((x^n − α)/g)^†`, so
`C^{⊥_H} ⊆ C ⟺ ((x^n−α)/g)^† ∈ (g) ⟺ ℓ := lcm(g, ((x^n−α)/g)^†) ∣ x^n − α` with `deg ℓ = deg g`,
i.e. `⟺ h(C) = n − deg g` (`CONSTITUENT_ENUMERATOR_FORMULA.md` Lemma 4).

In the enumeration the criterion is **not** applied by formula: `field_is_dc` tests the dual basis
against the code basis by linear algebra, independently of the lcm expression. The two agree on every
code enumerated (checked implicitly: the DC sets used in P3-4 and P3-6 are the same, and the counts
below reproduce the `(#DC)^e` totals of `P3_4_QUANTUM_DISTRIBUTION.md`).

## 3. What is *not* claimed here

* **Not a new criterion.** It is the source's own Theorem 15/16, restated componentwise via Theorem 1.
  Classification `K` (see `RESEARCH_GAP_AND_CONTRIBUTION_DECISION.md` §2, row 4). R3 died precisely
  because this enumeration is a product of published field-level counts.
* **Not a count of *useful* codes.** `K = 0` (zero logical qudits) and `K = en` (whole space) rows are
  included in the CSV for completeness and are flagged; neither is useful.
* **No optimality claim.** The quantum Singleton slack is recorded per row so a reader can see which
  rows are MDS, but at these toy lengths MDS is nearly automatic — see
  `QUANTUM_PARAMETER_VERIFICATION.md` §4, where the trivial cases are separated out.

## 4. Independence of the three Phase 6 questions — the separation the blueprint demands

The blueprint warns that these must not be conflated, and the project genuinely separates them:

| Question | Determined by | Independent of |
|---|---|---|
| hull dimension distribution | multiplicative over components (`CONSTITUENT_ENUMERATOR_FORMULA.md`) | distance |
| dual-containing | componentwise criterion (this document) | distance, and of hull dimension *value* |
| quantum parameters | hull dimension **and** `d(ψ)`, the latter coupling components (`P3_3_RING_GRAY.md` Thm 5) | — |

The third is the only one that couples the components. That is why it is the only one with content,
and why the other two are recorded as `K`.

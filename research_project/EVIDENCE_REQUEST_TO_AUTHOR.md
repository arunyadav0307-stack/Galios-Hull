## Draft e-mail

**Subject:** Request for one passage of the published version — "Galois hulls of
constacyclic codes over affine algebra rings", Discrete Math. 349(2) (2026) 114750

Dear Professor Prakash,

I am carrying out an independent verification of results in your article *"Galois hulls
of constacyclic codes over affine algebra rings"*, **Discrete Mathematics 349(2) (2026),
Article 114750, DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2025.114750**, and I would be grateful for your help
with a single passage of the published version, which I have not been able to consult.

**Why I am writing rather than simply reading the paper.** The article is not open
access, and I have no institutional subscription, so I can see only the abstract, the
introduction and the section snippets on the publisher's site. I do have the arXiv
preprint (arXiv:2412.08512v1, 11 December 2024). I am aware that the published version
differs from that preprint — it has 45 references rather than 44, it adds Example 4 and
Table 2, and the theorem numbering has changed (for instance the published Theorem 4
corresponds to Theorem 2 of the preprint). **So the preprint cannot be used as a
substitute for the published text**, which is exactly why I am asking you.

**The passage I need.** In §2 of the preprint, at the point where the constacyclic codes
over 𝒜 are introduced, one reads (preprint p. 7):

> "Since A = ∑_{S(ν)∈Ŝ} (e_{S(ν)} F_q), each element a ∈ A can be expressed as
> a = ∑_{S(ν)∈Ŝ} a_{S(ν)} e_{S(ν)}, **where a_{S(ν)} ∈ F_q for all S(ν) ∈ Ŝ**."

Taken together with the earlier statements in the same section — that the size of each
class is `|S(ν)| = lcm(d_1,…,d_ℓ) = [F_q(ν_1,…,ν_ℓ) : F_q]` (preprint p. 4) and that
`e_{S(ν)}R ≅ F_q(ν_1,…,ν_ℓ)` (preprint p. 5) — the coefficient field in that sentence
is the point I need to pin down, because the two possible readings differ by the factor
`|S(ν)|` in `dim_q 𝒜` and hence in the dimensions attributed to the codes later in the
paper.

I am **not** suggesting that the published version contains this, or any, error. The
published text may well have been revised at exactly this point; that is what I am
trying to establish. Nor am I asking you to endorse any conclusion of mine.

**What would settle it.** Any one of the following is enough:

1. **A copy of the published article** (a PDF reprint of the version of record is
   ideal), or
2. **A transcription or photograph of that one paragraph** — the sentence in §2 that
   gives the expansion `a = ∑_{S(ν)∈Ŝ} a_{S(ν)} e_{S(ν)}` and states the field the
   coefficients `a_{S(ν)}` belong to, with the page number, or
3. **A one-line answer**, if that is simplest. For the sentence in question, is it:

   - **(A)** `a_{S(ν)} ∈ F_q` for all `S(ν) ∈ Ŝ` — as in the preprint; or
   - **(B)** `a_{S(ν)} ∈ A_{S(ν)}`, equivalently `a_{S(ν)} ∈ F_{q^{|S(ν)|}}` — in which
     case the summands are the component fields rather than copies of `F_q`; or
   - **(C)** neither, because the passage was reorganised — in which case a short
     quotation would be very helpful.

**Secondary items, only if they are easy to include.** These are not needed to answer
the main question, but would let me check the downstream statements against the
published text rather than the preprint:

- the published statement corresponding to `C_{S(ν)} = {c_{S(ν)} ∈ F_q^n | c ∈ C}`
  (preprint eq. (4), p. 7);
- the published statement corresponding to eq. (10) on p. 17, and whether the Gray map
  is still described as a "bijective linear isometry" from `𝒜^n` to `F_q^{nN}`;
- whether Tables 1 and 2 and the new Example 4 still list the Gray images `ψ(C)` with
  length `2n`.

**What I will do with your reply.** I am preparing a private technical note recording

article, will not circulate it, and will not publish anything that misrepresents it. If

Thank you very much for your time. If it is easier for a co-author or your institution's

## A. Ambient arithmetic and fields

1. `p` is prime, `e >= 1`, and `q=p^e`.
2. The base field is `F_q`.
3. The square-free affine algebra has a fixed labeled decomposition
   `A ~= product_(s=1)^N K_s`, where
   `K_s=F_(q^(m_s))=F_(p^(d_s))` and `d_s=e m_s`, with `m_s>=1`.
4. Component labels are fixed. No component permutation, ring automorphism, code isometry, or equivalence quotient is taken in the main count.
5. The affine presentation, when used, is
   `A=F_q[X_1,...,X_l]/<t_1(X_1),...,t_l(X_l)>`, with `l>=1`, each `t_i` monic, square-free, and of positive degree. The positive-degree condition prevents the zero quotient exception.
6. The symbol `A` is the preferred ambient affine algebra. The legacy symbol `R` is not used for the main theorem; if retained in a citation comparison, it must be defined locally and must not be confused with `rho`.

## B. Length, twists, and simple-root hypotheses

7. `n>=1` and `gcd(n,p)=1`.
8. Each component twist `lambda_s` lies in `K_s^*`.
9. `M_s(x)=x^n-lambda_s` is therefore square-free/simple-root over `K_s`.
10. Let `mathcal F_s` be the set of distinct monic irreducible factors of `M_s` over `K_s`.
11. A component code is the principal ideal generated by a product of selected factors. The selected subset is `J_s subseteq mathcal F_s` and
    `g_(J_s)=product_(f in J_s) f`, with check polynomial
    `h_(J_s)=M_s/g_(J_s)`.
12. The global code is the labeled product of the component choices. The selection space is the Cartesian product of all subset spaces `J_s subseteq mathcal F_s`.

## C. Frozen code-first convention

13. The Galois parameter satisfies `0<=k<e`. It is a Frobenius iteration number, not the field exponent itself.
14. On `K_s`, define
    `sigma_(s,k)(a)=a^(p^k)` and
    `rho_(s,k)(a)=a^(p^(d_s-k))=sigma_(s,k)^(-1)`.
15. The code-first second-slot pairing is
    ` <x,y>_(s,k)=sum_i x_i sigma_(s,k)(y_i)=sum_i x_i y_i^(p^k)`.
16. The code-first dual is
    `D_code-first(C)={y in K_s^n: <c,y>_(s,k)=0 for every c in C}`.
17. The normalized code-first reciprocal of a monic polynomial `f(x)=sum_(i=0)^d f_i x^i`, `f_0 != 0`, is
    `f^(#_(s,k))(x)=rho_(s,k)(f_0)^(-1) sum_(i=0)^d rho_(s,k)(f_i)x^(d-i)`.
18. The code-first dual twist is `lambda_s^(-p^(d_s-k))`.
19. The same-factor-set compatibility condition is
    `lambda_s^(1+p^(d_s-k))=1` for every `s`.
20. The candidate-first comparison dual is defined separately by
    `D_candidate(C)={y: sum_i y_i sigma_(s,k)(c_i)=0 for every c in C}`.
21. The convention-transformation proposition is separate from the main support theorem and states
    `D_candidate(C)=sigma_(s,k)^2(D_code-first(C))`.
22. Candidate-first and code-first dual codes, hull subspaces, factor supports, dimensions, LCD claims, and enumerators must not be identified without the stated theorem or condition. The restricted Gram result gives equality of same-code hull dimensions and LCD decisions, not equality of subspaces or supports.

## D. Factor orbits and weights

23. Under compatibility, `tau_(s,k)(f)=f^(#_(s,k))` is a permutation of `mathcal F_s`.
24. Let `mathcal O_s` be the set of cycles of `tau_(s,k)`. For `O in mathcal O_s`, write its length as `a_O` and index it as `(f_0,...,f_(a_O-1))` with `tau(f_i)=f_(i+1)` cyclically.
25. All factors in one orbit have the same `K_s`-degree; write that degree as `d_O`.
26. The global `F_q` weight of an orbit position is `w_O=m_s d_O`.
27. For `epsilon_i in {0,1}`, `epsilon_i=1` means that `f_i` is selected in the generator factor set `J_s`.
28. The code-dimension contribution is `w_O(1-epsilon_i)` because a selected generator factor removes that factor degree from the quotient code dimension.
29. The code-first hull-support statistic is
    `b_O(epsilon)=sum_(i=0)^(a_O-1) epsilon_i(1-epsilon_(i+1))`, with cyclic indices.
30. The hull-dimension contribution is `w_O b_O(epsilon)`.

## E. Main enumerator hypotheses and interpretation

31. The principal enumerator assumes independent labeled selections across components and factor orbits.
32. Define
    `T_w(u,z)=[[u^w,1],[u^w z^w,1]]`.
33. The variable `u` records global `F_q`-code dimension; `z` records global code-first `F_q`-hull dimension.
34. The exact labeled joint polynomial is
    `E(u,z)=product_s product_(O in mathcal O_s) trace(T_(w_O)(u,z)^(a_O))`.
35. Equivalently,
    `E(u,z)=sum_(J_1,...,J_N) u^(K(C(J))) z^(H_k(C(J)))`, where the sum is over all distinct labeled factor selections.
36. `[u^K z^H]E(u,z)` counts selections with code dimension `K` and code-first hull dimension `H`.
37. At `u=1`, the hull polynomial is a product of orbit polynomials. At `u=z=1`, the total is `2^(sum_s |mathcal F_s|)`.
38. The orbit polynomial is
    `P_(a,w)(z)=trace(T_w(1,z)^a)=2+sum_(b=1)^(floor(a/2)) 2 binom(a,2b) z^(bw)`
    and equivalently
    `2+sum_(b=1)^(floor(a/2)) (a/b) binom(a-1,2b-1) z^(bw)`.
39. The two-cycle variance exception is retained: an orbit of length `2` contributes variance `w^2/4`; an orbit of length `a>=3` contributes `a w^2/16`; length `1` contributes zero.

## F. Scope exclusions

The following are excluded from the central theorem and must be stated in the manuscript limitations:

- repeated-root cases, including `p|n` or multiplicity-sensitive factor ideals;
- incompatible two-modulus twists, where the dual lies over `x^n-lambda_s^(-p^(d_s-k))` and the same-factor-set transfer is invalid;
- equivalence-class, isometry-class, automorphism-orbit, or quotient enumeration;
- Burnside/Pólya enumeration;
- unrestricted finite-ring code families beyond the stated reduced square-free affine setting;
- unconditional quantum-code parameters, quantum distance, optimality, or fault-tolerance conclusions;
- any inference of quantum distance from hull dimension alone;
- the underspecified N1 artifact.

## G. Computation and evidence boundary

40. A finite validator is evidence for its listed instance only; it is not a general proof.
41. Manuscript examples must specify the field, `n`, `k`, twist, factorization/orbit data, selection range, command, and captured output.
42. The permitted examples are the fully specified F4, F8, F16, compatible nontrivial-twist, N2-A, and incompatible N2-B diagnostics already recorded in the Phase-7 specification and validator captures.

## H. Claim boundary

The safe contribution is a conditional, self-contained exact joint labeled enumerator for the stated family.
